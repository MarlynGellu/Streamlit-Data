# Talang.in

> **Smart group finance management powered by Natural Language Processing.**

## About the Project

Splitting the bill should be easy, but tracking who owes what after a group trip or dinner often becomes a mathematical nightmare. **Talang.in** solves this by letting users type exactly what happened in Indonesian, and our NLP engine handles the rest. 

Instead of manually assigning amounts to each person, you just tell Talang.in the scenario, and it automatically creates the expense, splits the bill, and updates group balances.
